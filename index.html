about

I'm a software developer with 7+ years of experience building web and .NET applications across the energy and healthcare sectors.

My personal work focuses on privacy-first communication and open source — centered on the Matrix ecosystem, building encrypted chat clients, push delivery infrastructure, and tools that give people control over their own data.

Beyond messaging, I build game systems and mods with deep progression mechanics, testing rigor, and networked multiplayer state. I've also contributed upstream to flutter-webrtc and the Matrix Dart SDK. I care about clean architecture, thorough test coverage, and shipping software that respects users.

Current stack: Dart/Flutter, C#/.NET, TypeScript, and whatever the project needs.

BAIRN

An open source, privacy-first baby monitor — a free alternative to Cribside. Streams live video peer-to-peer over WebRTC so the feed never passes through a third-party server. Built with Flutter, targeting Android, iOS, web, and desktop.
