about

I'm a software developer focused on privacy-first communication and open source. My work centers on the Matrix ecosystem — building encrypted chat clients, push delivery infrastructure, and tools that give people control over their own data.


Beyond messaging, I build game systems and mods with deep progression mechanics, testing rigor, and networked multiplayer state. I care about clean architecture, thorough test coverage, and shipping software that respects users.


Current stack: Dart/Flutter, C#/.NET, TypeScript, and whatever the project needs.
